Things to do

Pick one, or do nothing at all. Both are popular.

Snorkel the reef

Calm, shallow water just off South Beach. Gear rentals are available at the dive shop.

Hike to the lighthouse

A 90-minute trail through palm forest, ending with a view of the whole island.

Sunset sail

Two-hour trips leave from the village dock every evening, weather permitting.
