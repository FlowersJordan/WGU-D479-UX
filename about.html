A small island with a big range

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific, with less than 500 square miles to explore. Within that space you will find sandy and rocky beaches, a small but safe harbor, lush rainforest, and a mountainous interior that includes an active volcano.

About 20,000 Tanitians call the island home. Fishing and farming carried the local economy for generations, and tourism is the newest chapter. You are visiting a place that is still very much lived in.

  • <500 square miles of island
  • 20,000 Tanitian residents
  • 1 small, active volcano

Where to spend your time

Most visitors come for the beaches, the rainforest, and the volcano. Here is where to find them.

Taniti City

Native architecture and white, sandy beaches that circle Yellow Leaf Bay. It is flat and very walkable, and it is where most visitors spend their time.

Merriton Landing

A fast-growing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay. Many of the island's activities are here, and it is easy to explore on foot, so many travelers stay nearby.

Rainforest and volcano

Hike through the rainforest, zip-line through the canopy, or visit Taniti's active volcano in the mountainous interior.

Getting here, getting around, and settling in

The short version of what you need to plan a trip.

Getting here

Almost every visitor arrives by air at Taniti's small airport. A small cruise ship also docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

Getting around

Public buses serve Taniti City, taxis are available there, and rental cars and bikes are easy to find.

Where to stay

Lodging runs from an inexpensive hostel to one large four-star resort, with family-owned hotels and bed and breakfasts in between. All lodging is government regulated and regularly inspected.

Where to eat

Ten restaurants serve local fish and rice, American-style meals, and Pan-Asian cuisine. For groceries there are two supermarkets, two smaller stores, and a convenience store open 24 hours a day.

Good to know before you go

A few practical things that make the first day easier.

Money

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and major credit cards are widely taken. Several banks can exchange currency.

Power

Outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. In rural areas, especially among older residents, very little English is spoken.

Health

There is one hospital, with many multilingual staff, and several clinics.

Safety

Violent crime is very rare. As visitor numbers grow, petty theft such as pickpocketing is becoming more common, so keep an eye on your belongings in busy areas.

Holidays

Taniti has a large number of national holidays, and many attractions and restaurants close for them. Check dates before you finalize plans.

Alcohol

Alcohol is not served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m. The legal drinking age is 18.
