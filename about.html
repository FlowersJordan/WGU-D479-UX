A small island, on purpose

Taniti Island is only seven kilometers long. You can walk from the north lighthouse to the southern reef in an afternoon, and most visitors do. Boats from the mainland arrive twice a day, which keeps the crowds small.

Our village has a market, a bakery, and a dive shop. Everything else is beach, jungle, and sky.

  • 3 beaches
  • 95°F average water temperature
  • 45 min by boat from the mainland
