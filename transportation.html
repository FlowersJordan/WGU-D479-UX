Getting to Taniti

Almost every visitor arrives by air. A cruise ship stops by once a week.

By air

Taniti's small airport handles small jets and propeller planes. An expansion is underway so that larger jets can land within the next few years.

By cruise ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

Getting around

Taniti City is easy without a car. Beyond it, you will want a bus, a bike, or a rental.

Public buses

Buses serve Taniti City and run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

Taxis

Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Rental cars

A local rental agency near the airport rents cars, so you can pick one up the day you land.

Bikes

Several vendors rent bikes and helmets. Helmets are required by law.

On foot

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. The area around Merriton Landing, where many visitors stay, is also easy to explore on foot.
